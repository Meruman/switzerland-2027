Central Switzerland

Lucerne, lakes & legendary peaks

A fjord-like lake ringed by famous peaks: Pilatus, Rigi, Stoos, Bürgenstock. One pass covers almost all of it, and Lucerne is only 5 hours from home.

At a glance

Why this trip

Pilatus opened on 11 May in 2026. The Stoos ridge hike is usually snow-free from mid-June.

  • 2Lucerne is the closest Alpine base to Langenfeld (≈ 5 h), so you lose less holiday to travel.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

Lucerne

Langenfeld → Basel → Lucerne (≈ 5 h). Chapel Bridge, Lion Monument and the free Musegg wall towers.

Hotel guests get the Lucerne Visitor Card: free city buses.
Day2

Pilatus Golden Round Trip

Boat → Alpnachstad → the world's steepest cog railway → Pilatus Kulm → gondola down to Kriens.

Day3

Rigi, Queen of the Mountains

Boat to Vitznau → cog railway to Rigi Kulm → walk to Kaltbad and the panorama trail to Rigi Scheidegg.

Splurge: Mario Botta's Rigi Kaltbad mineral baths.
Day4

Stoos ridge

Steepest funicular in the world (110 %) → chairlift to Klingenstock → ridge hike to Fronalpstock (≈ 2.5 h) with views of 10 lakes.

Needs a head for heights and snow-free conditions (usually from mid-June).
Day5

Engelberg

Trübsee alpine lake and the Engstlenalp trail, or Brunni and the Härzlisee. Titlis is extra (glacier, Cliff Walk).

Day6

Bürgenstock & Stanserhorn

Boat + funicular to Bürgenstock, the cliff path to the Hammetschwand lift (Europe's highest outdoor lift). Afternoon: the CabriO open-top cable car up Stanserhorn.

Day7

Hidden gem day

Maderanertal: waterfalls, the Golzernsee lake and a historic hotel. Or Seelisberg → the Rütli meadow, birthplace of Switzerland, by boat on Lake Uri.

Day8

Home

Morning on the lake, train home in the afternoon (≈ 5 h).

Getting there & around

From your door to the mountains

Langenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Lucerne

Getting there

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel → Lucerne (≈ 5 h, one change). DB Sparpreis Europa goes straight to Luzern.

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy all 5 seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • 💡 Domestic tickets to Basel Bad Bf open 12 months ahead and are often cheapest. Basel SBB is one stop on.
  • 💡 DB group fares start at 6 people, and a BahnCard only discounts the German part.

Getting around

Lucerne Travel Pass: 3 days CHF 240, 5 days CHF 290, 10 days CHF 365 (one price all year, no extra discounts). It includes Pilatus, Rigi, Stoos, Bürgenstock, Stanserhorn, Brunni and the lake boats. For 7 days, buy the 5-day pass and do Lucerne-only days without it.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • The Stoos ridge trail is narrow in places, so it is not for people with vertigo. The Fronalpstock summit is a gentler alternative.
  • Ascension (6 May) and Whit Monday (17 May) bring Swiss day-trippers. Book boats and lifts early in the day.
  • Lake Lucerne is chilly for swimming until late June (≈ 17–19 °C).
  • Pilatus (cog railway) usually opens mid-May. Before that, use the Kriens gondola.

Worth paying for

  • Titlis≈ CHF 100+ full priceGlacier, ice cave and Cliff Walk at 3,020 m.
  • Rigi Kaltbad spa≈ CHF 40A mineral bath designed by Mario Botta, with alpine views.
  • Bürgenstock lunch≈ CHF 60–100Lunch at the luxury resort's terrace above the lake.
