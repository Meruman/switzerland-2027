Ticino

Emerald rivers, palm trees & stone villages

Switzerland with an Italian soul: jade-green rivers, waterfalls, stone villages and lakeside piazzas, with free public transport for hotel guests.

At a glance

Why this trip

The best region for May: 20–25 °C, rivers full, before the summer heat. River water is still cold (≈ 12–16 °C).

  • 1Ticino Ticket: stay at any of 500+ hotels, hostels or campsites and all trains and buses in the canton are free, with 20–30 % off cable cars.
  • 2Warm and green in May, when the high Alps are still snowy.
  • 3Valle Verzasca and Valle Maggia: the 'Swiss Maldives' rivers and 110 m waterfalls.
  • 4Grotto dinners (rustic stone taverns) are cheap, delicious and very local.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

South through the Gotthard

Langenfeld → Basel → Gotthard Base Tunnel → Bellinzona → Tenero/Locarno (≈ 8 h). Aperitivo on Lake Maggiore. Friends from Mexico City: fly into Milan Malpensa (Aeroméxico nonstop from spring 2027), then ≈ 2 h by train to Locarno with one change. Zürich works too (≈ 2 h 45 through the Gotthard).

Day2

Valle Verzasca

Bus 321 to Sonogno, hike the riverside Sentierone down to Lavertezzo (≈ 3–4 h) and the Ponte dei Salti double-arch bridge. Stop at the stone village of Corippo.

Go on a weekday and start early. Lavertezzo is busy at weekends.
Day3

Valle Maggia & Bavona

Bus to Bignasco → Val Bavona: Foroglio's 110 m waterfall and stone hamlets with no mains electricity. Mario Botta's church in Mogno.

The Bavona road reopened in April 2025 after the 2024 flood.
Day4

Cardada & Ascona

Funicular + cable car + chairlift to Cimetta: from Monte Rosa down to the lake. Afternoon in Ascona.

Day5

Centovalli or islands

The Centovalli railway to Verdasio, the little cable car up to car-free Rasa and a walk down to Intragna. Or a boat to the Brissago Islands botanical gardens.

Day6

Bellinzona → Lugano

UNESCO castles of Bellinzona (Castelgrande), then on to Lugano.

Day7

Lake Lugano

Monte Brè funicular → olive trail to Gandria → boat back. Sunset on Monte San Salvatore.

Day8

Monte Generoso or Como

Cog railway to Monte Generoso (Mario Botta's 'stone flower' summit building) or a day trip to Como / Bellagio in Italy (30 min).

Morcote by boat is a lovely calmer option.
Day9

Home

Lugano → Zürich → Köln (≈ 7.5 h). Friends: from Lugano it is ≈ 1 h 30 by direct train to Malpensa.

Getting there & around

Two routes, one meeting point

You twoLangenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Tenero / Locarno
FriendsMexico City→✈️ Milan Malpensa (MXP)→Tenero / Locarno

You two, from Langenfeld

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel → Lucerne/Arth-Goldau → Gotthard Base Tunnel → Bellinzona → Locarno (≈ 8 h). Cheapest: DB Sparpreis to Basel + an SBB Saver Day Pass (from CHF 52, or CHF 29 with Half Fare) for the Swiss part each way.

Friends, from Mexico City

Fly into Milan Malpensa (Aeroméxico nonstop from spring 2027), then ≈ 2 h by train to Locarno with one change. Zürich works too (≈ 2 h 45 through the Gotthard). From Lugano it is ≈ 1 h 30 by direct train to Malpensa.

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy both seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • ✈️ No nonstop to Switzerland: one stop via Madrid, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt or Munich. Book 3–6 months ahead.
  • ✈️ Nonstop Mexico City – Milan Malpensa with Aeroméxico from spring 2027, handy for the Ticino and Italian-lakes end of a trip.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • River currents and sudden water releases from dams are dangerous. Swim only at calm, marked spots, never above rapids.
  • Corpus Christi (27 May 2027) is a holiday in Ticino, and Italian tourists arrive on long weekends.
  • Ticino Ticket eligibility varies: most hotels, hostels and campsites take part, but many Airbnbs do not. Ask before booking.
  • Mosquito repellent helps by the lakes in the evening.

Worth paying for

  • GoldenEye bungee jump≈ CHF 200+The 220 m Verzasca Dam jump from the James Bond film.
  • Monte Generoso dinner≈ CHF 60–90Sunset dinner in Mario Botta's Fiore di Pietra.
  • Boat day on Lake Maggiore≈ CHF 30–50To Italian Cannobio market (Sundays).
