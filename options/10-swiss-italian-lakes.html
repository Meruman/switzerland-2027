Swiss–Italian Lakes

Bernina, Lake Como & Lugano

Swiss mountains, Italian lakes and prices: ride the Bernina Express from Tirano, sleep cheaper on Lake Como and in Chiavenna, and visit Soglio, 'the threshold of paradise'.

At a glance

Why this trip

Lakes are lovely from May. For Bernina lifts (Muottas Muragl, Diavolezza) go after ~6–20 June. Avoid August.

  • 1Half of the nights are in Italy: apartments, pizza and espresso cost much less.
  • 2The Bernina Express from Tirano is the cheapest way to ride the most beautiful train in Switzerland.
  • 3Lake Como's villas, ferries and the old Wayfarer's Path (Sentiero del Viandante).
  • 4Hidden gems: Soglio, Chiavenna's crotti cave taverns, the Acquafraggia waterfalls.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

To Lugano

Langenfeld → Basel → Gotthard Base Tunnel → Lugano (≈ 7.5 h). Lakeside walk in Parco Ciani.

Day2

Lugano

Monte Brè → olive trail to Gandria → boat back. Evening on Monte San Salvatore.

Day3

To Lake Como

Bus via Porlezza to Menaggio (≈ 1 h), ferry to Varenna. Castello di Vezio at sunset.

Day4

Ferry-hopping

Bellagio, Villa Carlotta's gardens (Tremezzo), Villa del Balbianello (Star Wars and Bond).

The mid-lake ferry day ticket saves money.
Day5

Wayfarer's Path

Hike the Sentiero del Viandante Varenna → Bellano (≈ 2.5 h) and the Orrido di Bellano gorge. Swim at a lido.

Day6

Bernina Express

Train Varenna → Tirano (≈ 1.5 h, cheap Italian fare), then the Bernina line over the Brusio spiral viaduct, Alp Grüm and Lago Bianco → Pontresina.

Regional trains on the same line need no reservation.
Day7

Glaciers

Morteratsch glacier walk, Muottas Muragl panorama or Diavolezza, and the Lej da Staz forest lake.

Day8

Maloja → Soglio → Chiavenna

PostBus down the Maloja hairpins → Soglio (chestnut forest trail to Castasegna) → Chiavenna, Italy.

The seasonal 'Palm Express' bus follows this road. Reservation needed.
Day9

Chiavenna

Acquafraggia twin waterfalls, Lake Mezzola, and dinner in a crotto (natural cave cellar tavern).

Day10

Slow boat down Lake Como

Train to Colico, then a slow ferry the whole length of the lake to Como (≈ 4 h), and the train to Lugano (≈ 30 min).

Day11

Home

Lugano → Zürich → Köln (≈ 7.5 h).

Getting there & around

From your door to the mountains

Langenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Lugano

Getting there

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel → Lugano via the Gotthard Base Tunnel (≈ 7.5 h). Ticket: DB Sparpreis Europa to Lugano, or DB to Basel + an SBB Saver Day Pass.

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy all 5 seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • 💡 Domestic tickets to Basel Bad Bf open 12 months ahead and are often cheapest. Basel SBB is one stop on.
  • 💡 DB group fares start at 6 people, and a BahnCard only discounts the German part.

Getting around

No pass. Italian trains and ferries are cheap (Varenna → Tirano ≈ €8). The Swiss legs (Bernina, PostBus, Lugano) ≈ CHF 120–180, and half that with a Half Fare Card if you add more Swiss days.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • You cross into Italy and back: bring passports/ID, and note the euro vs franc split.
  • Lake Como ferries are busy at weekends. Morning departures are calmer.
  • Italian restaurants charge 'coperto' (cover charge) of €2–4 per person. That's normal.
  • Mobile data: EU roaming works in Italy, but Switzerland may cost extra on German tariffs.

Worth paying for

  • Villa del Balbianello≈ €25–30The most famous villa garden on the lake.
  • Private boat on Lake Como≈ €150–250 per boatSplit between 5 it's worth it for 2 hours.
  • Crotto dinner≈ €35–50Bresaola, pizzoccheri and local wine.
