At a glance

Why this trip

High trails (Five Lakes Walk, Aletsch ridge) are usually snow-free from mid or late June.

  • 3The Aletsch Glacier (UNESCO), 20 km of ice seen from a car-free plateau.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

To the glacier village

Langenfeld → Basel → Bern → Visp → bus to Saas-Fee (≈ 7 h). Collect your SaastalCard at check-in.

Day2

Free lifts day 1

Spielboden & Längfluh lifts (free) for close-up glacier views. Feed the marmots at Spielboden. Walk down via the glacier trail.

Day3

Hohsaas 4000er trail

Saas-Grund → Kreuzboden → Hohsaas (3,200 m, free): the '18 four-thousanders' view trail. Afternoon: Kapellenweg chapel path to Saas-Fee.

The Metro Alpin to 3,500 m is not free (≈ CHF 45 with the card).
Day4

Mattmark & Saas-Almagell

Bus to the Mattmark lake (2,200 m) and walk around the turquoise reservoir, or take the Heidbodme lift and do the Almagell high trail.

Day5

To the Matterhorn

Bus + train to Täsch (≈ 1 h). Afternoon in Zermatt village and the Gornerschlucht gorge.

Shuttle Täsch ⇄ Zermatt: CHF 17.20 return.
Day6

Gornergrat & Riffelsee

Gornergrat cog railway (CHF 132, or CHF 66 with Half Fare). Walk down via Rotenboden → Riffelsee → Riffelberg for the reflection shot.

Or the Five Lakes Walk (Sunnegga–Blauherd combo CHF 53) if snow-free.
Day7

To the Aletsch plateau

Täsch → Visp → Mörel → cable car to car-free Riederalp (≈ 1.5 h). Walk through the ancient Aletsch forest.

Day8

Aletsch Glacier day

Moosfluh → panoramic trail along the glacier edge → Bettmerhorn or Eggishorn: the longest glacier in the Alps right below you.

Trails on the Moosfluh slope are monitored and can be rerouted, so check locally.
Day9

Spa day in Leukerbad

Day trip to Leukerbad (≈ 1.5 h): open-air thermal pools facing 1,000 m cliffs, plus the Gemmi pass cable car.

Day10

Home

Brig → Bern → Basel → Köln (≈ 6.5 h).

Getting there & around

From your door to the mountains

Langenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Saas-Fee / Saas-Grund

Getting there

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel → Bern → Visp (≈ 6 h), then the PostBus up the Saas valley. DB sells Sparpreis Europa tickets to Visp/Brig. Home from Brig.

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy all 5 seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • 💡 Domestic tickets to Basel Bad Bf open 12 months ahead and are often cheapest. Basel SBB is one stop on.
  • 💡 DB group fares start at 6 people, and a BahnCard only discounts the German part.

Getting around

A Swiss Half Fare Card (CHF 150, 1 month) is best here, because the Saas lifts are free anyway. Expect ≈ CHF 190 more in half-price tickets (Gornergrat, Aletsch lifts, Leukerbad, transfers). The Swiss Travel Pass would cost more for this route.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • Zermatt and Saas-Fee are car-free. Cars park in Täsch or at the Saas-Fee entrance.
  • In June 2024 floods hit parts of Valais (Saas valley and Zermatt were briefly cut off). Check alerts before travelling.
  • The Five Lakes Walk and Aletsch high trails open when the snow melts, often only in late June.
  • Valais has the most sunshine in Switzerland, but it is still the Alps: pack layers.

Worth paying for

  • Matterhorn Glacier Paradise≈ CHF 100+ with discountsEurope's highest cable car station (3,883 m).
  • Night on Gornergrat≈ CHF 200+ ppSunrise on 29 four-thousand-metre peaks.
  • Leukerbad spa evening≈ CHF 30–40Hot pools under the stars.
