Appenzell & the East

Hidden gems close to home

The cliff-side Aescher guesthouse, mirror lakes in the Alpstein, Europe's mightiest waterfall and a village you can only reach by boat. Cheap, close and still a secret.

🗓 8 days · 7 nights☀️ Best 1 June – 31 July💶 ≈ €1 090–1 790 pp🚆 ≈ 5–6 h by train

At a glance

Why this trip

Lower mountains (max ~2,000 m) open early. The new Säntis cable car opens in spring 2027. July is just as good and barely pricier.

  • 1One of the cheapest options here: lower prices than the big resorts, and no pass needed.
  • 2Seealpsee, Aescher, Fälensee: the Alpstein is a compact hiking paradise.
  • 3Rhine Falls, painted Stein am Rhein, the St. Gallen Abbey Library: culture on the way.
  • 4Walensee and car-free Quinten feel like a secret Swiss Riviera.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

Rhine Falls

Langenfeld → Köln → Singen/Zürich → Schaffhausen (≈ 5 h). Rhine Falls at evening light, with a boat to the rock in the middle.

Day2

Stein am Rhein → Appenzell

Painted houses of Stein am Rhein, then St. Gallen's UNESCO Abbey Library and on to Appenzell.

Day3

Seealpsee & Aescher

Cable car to Ebenalp → Wildkirchli caves → Aescher cliff guesthouse → steep path down to Seealpsee → Wasserauen (≈ 3 h).

Ebenalp cable car ≈ CHF 24 one way.
Day4

Alpstein lakes

Brülisau → Sämtisersee → Fälensee (a fjord-like mirror lake) → Bollenwees → back (≈ 5 h). Fit hikers start from Hoher Kasten along the Stauberen ridge.

Day5

Appenzell village

Painted façades, the Appenzeller show dairy in Stein, and the Barfussweg barefoot trail Gontenbad → Appenzell (≈ 1.5 h).

Säntis: the new cable car opens in spring 2027. Check prices (half-fare discount reportedly dropped).
Day6

To Walensee

Train to Walenstadt/Murg, boat to car-free Quinten (vineyards and fig trees under the Churfirsten cliffs).

Day7

Seerenbach & Klöntal

Seerenbach Falls, one of the highest waterfalls in Switzerland, or a trip to the turquoise Klöntalersee near Glarus.

Day8

Home

Zürich → Köln (≈ 5 h).

Getting there & around

From your door to the mountains

Langenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Schaffhausen

Getting there

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel or Stuttgart/Singen → Schaffhausen (≈ 5 h). Home from Zürich. Driving is also easy here (≈ 6 h), and Appenzell parking is cheap.

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy all 5 seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • 💡 Domestic tickets to Basel Bad Bf open 12 months ahead and are often cheapest. Basel SBB is one stop on.
  • 💡 DB group fares start at 6 people, and a BahnCard only discounts the German part.

Getting around

No pass needed. Buy individual tickets with Supersaver fares in the SBB app. The Half Fare Card (CHF 150) does not pay off on this short route. Lifts: Ebenalp CHF 36 return, Hoher Kasten extra.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • The descent from Aescher to Seealpsee is steep and slippery when wet. Wear proper shoes.
  • The Säntis cable car was rebuilt in 2026 (closed May to autumn 2026). Official reopening is spring 2027, so check before planning it.
  • Many mountain guesthouses take cash only. Bring some CHF.
  • Lake Constance and the German side (Lindau, Mainau) are an easy extra day.

Worth paying for

  • New Säntis cable carprice TBC (2027)The highest peak in the east, with a view over 6 countries.
  • Rhine Falls boat to the rock≈ CHF 15–20Climb the rock in the middle of Europe's most powerful waterfall.
