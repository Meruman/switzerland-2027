The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

To Lucerne

Langenfeld → Basel → Lucerne (≈ 5.5 h). Chapel Bridge, old town and the Musegg wall towers (free). Friends from Mexico City: fly into Zürich. The Swiss Travel Pass covers the 1 h train from the airport to Lucerne.

Day2

Queen of the Mountains

Boat to Vitznau and the cog railway up Rigi (free with the pass). Hike the panorama trail Rigi Kaltbad → Rigi Scheidegg.

Alternative: the Pilatus Golden Round Trip (50 % off with the pass).
Day3

Over the Brünig to the waterfalls

Lucerne–Interlaken Express over the Brünig pass, past three lakes. Afternoon at Staubbach and Trümmelbach Falls.

Day4

Mürren & Gimmelwald

Grütschalp → Mürren panorama path, then down to Gimmelwald.

Schilthorn is half price from Mürren with the pass (≈ CHF 46).
Day5

Eiger day

Grindelwald First / Bachalpsee, or the Männlichen → Kleine Scheidegg Royal Walk.

Jungfraujoch splurge: the pass gives 25 % off above Wengen/Grindelwald.
Day6

GoldenPass Express

Interlaken → Montreux in 3 h 15 through Gstaad's chalet country (reservation CHF 20). Château de Chillon is free with the pass.

Day7

Lavaux vineyards

Walk the UNESCO wine terraces from Chexbres down to St-Saphorin (≈ 2.5 h), taste wine at a caveau, and ride a Belle Époque paddle steamer back (free with the pass).

Day8

To the Matterhorn

Montreux → Visp → Täsch / Zermatt (≈ 2.5 h). Evening walk in car-free Zermatt.

Sleep in Täsch to save 30–40 %. The shuttle takes 12 minutes.
Day9

Gornergrat & Riffelsee

Cog railway to Gornergrat (CHF 66 with the pass). Get off at Rotenboden on the way down for the Matterhorn mirrored in Riffelsee.

Or the Five Lakes Walk once it is snow-free (usually late June).
Day10

Glacier Express

8 hours Zermatt → St. Moritz over the Oberalp pass and the Landwasser Viaduct. Reservation CHF 54.

Budget trick: regional trains on the same line need no reservation.
Day11

Bernina glaciers

Walk to the Morteratsch glacier, then Diavolezza (from ~20 June) or Muottas Muragl (from ~6 June) for the sunset view over the lakes.

Day12

Bernina Express to Italy

Pontresina → Alp Grüm → Brusio spiral viaduct → Tirano (≈ 2 h, reservation ≈ CHF 32), then the seasonal Bernina Express bus along Lake Como to Lugano.

Day13

Lugano dolce vita

Monte Brè funicular, the olive trail to Gandria, and a boat to Morcote, one of the prettiest villages in Switzerland.

Day14

Home

Lugano → Gotthard Base Tunnel → Zürich → Köln (≈ 7.5 h). Or splurge on the Gotthard Panorama Express (boat + train) to Lucerne. Friends: fly home from Milan Malpensa, ≈ 1 h 30 by train from Lugano (Aeroméxico flies nonstop to Mexico City from spring 2027), or from Zürich (≈ 2 h).

Getting there & around

Two routes, one meeting point

You twoLangenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Lucerne
FriendsMexico City→✈️ Zürich (ZRH)→Lucerne

You two, from Langenfeld

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel SBB (ICE ≈ 4 h) → Lucerne (1 h). Buy the DB ticket only as far as Basel SBB, because the Swiss Travel Pass covers everything from there. Home from Lugano via Zürich or Basel.

Friends, from Mexico City

Fly into Zürich. The Swiss Travel Pass covers the 1 h train from the airport to Lucerne. Fly home from Milan Malpensa, ≈ 1 h 30 by train from Lugano (Aeroméxico flies nonstop to Mexico City from spring 2027), or from Zürich (≈ 2 h).

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy both seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • ✈️ No nonstop to Switzerland: one stop via Madrid, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt or Munich. Book 3–6 months ahead.
  • ✈️ Nonstop Mexico City – Milan Malpensa with Aeroméxico from spring 2027, handy for the Ticino and Italian-lakes end of a trip.

Getting around

Swiss Travel Pass, 15 days, 2nd class: CHF 499 (youth CHF 356). It covers almost everything. Seat reservations are extra: GoldenPass Express CHF 20, Glacier Express CHF 54, Bernina Express ≈ CHF 32–44 and the Bernina bus. Budget ≈ CHF 140 per person for reservations. For the cheapest option, skip the reservations and use regional trains on the same lines.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • This is the most expensive and most 'moving-around' option, with six check-ins. Use SBB luggage forwarding (≈ CHF 12–25 per bag) to travel light.
  • Glacier Express seats sell out in June. Reservations reportedly open ~93 days ahead.
  • Zermatt is car-free and pricey. Täsch (one stop before) is the money saver.
  • The pass also gives free entry to 500+ museums, so use it on rainy days.

Worth paying for

  • Glacier Express Excellence Class+ CHF 540 reservationHonestly not worth it for your budget. The normal 2nd class has the same windows.
  • Jungfraujoch≈ CHF 177 with the passOnly on a clear day.
  • 1st-class pass15 days ≈ CHF 790Quieter carriages. Not needed in May or June.
