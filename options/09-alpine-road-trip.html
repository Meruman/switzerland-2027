At a glance

Why this trip

In 2026 the Furka, Grimsel and Nufenen passes opened on 29 May and Susten in mid-June. Go after mid-June. July adds snow-free high trails; August has the busiest pass roads.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

Rhine Falls → Lake Lucerne

Drive ≈ 5.5 h to the Rhine Falls (break), then on to Brunnen on Lake Lucerne. Friends from Mexico City: fly into Zürich. You two pick them up at the airport on the drive down (≈ 45 min to Lake Lucerne).

Fill up the tank in Germany. Fuel is cheaper.
Day2

Stoos

Steepest funicular in the world and the Klingenstock → Fronalpstock ridge. Drive the Axenstrasse along the lake.

Day3

Susten Pass

Over the Susten pass (glacier at the roadside) → Aare Gorge → Lake Brienz (Iseltwald) → Lauterbrunnen.

Susten usually opens mid-June.
Day4

Mürren & Trümmelbach

Leave the car and take the train/cable car to car-free Mürren. Trümmelbach Falls.

Day5

Grindelwald

First & Bachalpsee, or the Männlichen Royal Walk.

Day6

Oeschinensee → Valais

Oeschinensee + Blausee, then the Lötschberg car train Kandersteg → Goppenstein (≈ CHF 30 per car) → Täsch.

Day7

Matterhorn

Shuttle to Zermatt, then Gornergrat & Riffelsee or the Five Lakes Walk.

Day8

Furka & Oberalp

Rhône valley → Furka pass (Hotel Belvédère, Rhône Glacier ice grotto) → Andermatt → Oberalp lighthouse → Flims.

Day9

Caumasee & Rhine Gorge

Swim in the turquoise lakes, then the Il Spir viewpoint over the Ruinaulta.

Day10

San Bernardino → Ticino

Over the San Bernardino pass → Bellinzona castles → Tenero on Lake Maggiore.

Day11

Verzasca & Maggia

Verzasca riverside hike + Foroglio waterfall.

Day12

Gotthard Tremola

Drive the cobbled Tremola hairpins over the Gotthard → Andermatt → Lucerne area.

Day13

Home

≈ 6–7 h drive home. Friends: drop them at Zürich airport on the last day (≈ 45 min from Lucerne).

Getting there & around

Two routes, one meeting point

You twoLangenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Zürich airport→Lake Lucerne
FriendsMexico City→✈️ Zürich (ZRH)→Lake Lucerne

You two, from Langenfeld

You two drive down and pick the friends up at Zürich airport. Your own car is tight for 5 adults + luggage (add a roof box), so consider a 7-seater (VW Touran/Sharan, V-Class) from Düsseldorf or Köln for ≈ €50–100/day, usually cheaper than renting in Switzerland. Check the rental allows Switzerland. Vignette CHF 40 only at via.admin.ch (resellers charge more).

Friends, from Mexico City

Fly into Zürich. You two pick them up at the airport on the drive down (≈ 45 min to Lake Lucerne). Drop them at Zürich airport on the last day (≈ 45 min from Lucerne).

  • 💡 Buy the e-vignette only at via.admin.ch (CHF 40 per car). Resellers charge €50+.
  • 💡 Fill up in Germany and check the rental contract allows Switzerland and Italy.
  • ✈️ No nonstop to Switzerland: one stop via Madrid, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt or Munich. Book 3–6 months ahead.
  • ✈️ Nonstop Mexico City – Milan Malpensa with Aeroméxico from spring 2027, handy for the Ticino and Italian-lakes end of a trip.

Getting around

The car does most of the work. Budget ≈ CHF 200–350 per person for lifts and mountain trains (Mürren, First, Gornergrat, Oeschinensee). A Half Fare Card (CHF 150) roughly breaks even, so only buy it if you add Jungfraujoch or Schilthorn.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • Pass roads can close for a day even in June after snowfall. Check alpen-paesse.ch or TCS each morning.
  • Rules: headlights on during the day, and very high speeding fines. PostBuses have priority on mountain roads (three-tone horn).
  • Car-free villages (Mürren, Wengen, Zermatt, Saas-Fee): park in Lauterbrunnen/Täsch (≈ CHF 10–25/day).
  • Driving 2,000 km: share the driving, and take breaks at the many pass-top restaurants.

Worth paying for

  • Rhône Glacier ice grottosmall feeWalk inside a glacier (opens when the pass opens).
  • Rafting in the Rhine Gorge≈ CHF 120–150From Ilanz through the Ruinaulta.
  • Jungfraujoch≈ CHF 261 full / 131 Half FareOnly on a clear day.
