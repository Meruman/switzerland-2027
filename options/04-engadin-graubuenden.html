At a glance

Why this trip

Summer lifts open between ~6 and 27 June (2026: Muottas Muragl 6 June, Diavolezza 20 June). July and August have every lift, the warmest lakes and the most out of 'lifts included'.

  • 1'Hotel & Bergbahnen inklusive': many Upper Engadin hotels include mountain lifts (and often local transport) from the 2nd night.
  • 2The UNESCO Albula & Bernina railways: spiral tunnels, the Landwasser Viaduct and Lago Bianco.
  • 3Fewer crowds than the Jungfrau region and a real 'secret Switzerland' feel.
  • 4Caumasee, the Rhine Gorge and the Swiss National Park, Switzerland's only national park.

The plan

Day by day

Keep 1–2 days flexible and save the big mountain days for clear weather.

Day1

To Flims

Langenfeld → Basel/Zürich → Chur → bus to Flims (≈ 6.5 h). Evening walk to the forest lakes.

Day2

Caumasee & Rhine Gorge

Morning swim at turquoise Caumasee (a lift goes down to the beach, with an entry fee in summer) or the free, smaller Crestasee. Walk to the Il Spir platform above the Ruinaulta, the 'Swiss Grand Canyon'.

Day3

Albula railway

Chur → Filisur (Landwasser Viaduct viewpoint) → Bergün → spiral tunnels → Pontresina. Check in to a 'lifts included' hotel.

Day4

Morteratsch & Diavolezza

Glacier walk to the Morteratsch glacier tongue (signs show how far it has retreated), then Diavolezza for the Bernina massif.

Diavolezza opened on 20 June in 2026. Before that, do Muottas Muragl instead.
Day5

Sils & Val Fex

Car-free Val Fex by foot or horse carriage, the Chastè peninsula on Lake Sils, and the hidden forest lake Lej da Staz on the way back.

Day6

Bernina line to Italy

Regional train (no reservation needed) to Ospizio Bernina → walk along Lago Bianco to Alp Grüm (≈ 1.5 h) → continue to Tirano for Italian lunch and gelato.

Day7

To Lower Engadin

Train to Scuol. Afternoon at the Bogn Engiadina thermal spa.

Day8

Swiss National Park

Zernez visitor centre, then a trail in the park, e.g. Il Fuorn → Margunet, to spot chamois, ibex and marmots.

Day9

Guarda & Tarasp

Hike the 'Schellen-Ursli' village of Guarda with its sgraffito houses → Ardez, and visit fairy-tale Tarasp castle.

Day10

Home

Scuol → Vereina tunnel → Landquart → Zürich → Köln (≈ 7 h).

Getting there & around

From your door to the mountains

Langenfeld→Köln / Düsseldorf→Basel→Flims

Getting there

Langenfeld → Köln → Basel or Zürich → Chur (≈ 6 h). DB sells through-tickets to Chur. The Rhaetian Railway (RhB) takes over from there.

  • 💡 International DB tickets open 6 months ahead. Buy all 5 seats in one booking on day one.
  • 💡 'Super Sparpreis' can't be refunded. 'Sparpreis' can be cancelled before travel for a small fee.
  • 💡 Domestic tickets to Basel Bad Bf open 12 months ahead and are often cheapest. Basel SBB is one stop on.
  • 💡 DB group fares start at 6 people, and a BahnCard only discounts the German part.

Getting around

Half Fare Card (CHF 150) + tickets. In Upper Engadin most lifts and often buses are free with participating hotels. In Lower Engadin ask your host for the guest card. Budget ≈ CHF 120 more for half-price train tickets.

Before you go

Good to know & worth the splurge

Good to know

  • Engadin is at 1,800 m, so nights can be cold even in June. Bring a warm layer.
  • 'Lifts included' is per hotel and usually starts from the 2nd night. Confirm before booking.
  • In summer 2026 the St. Moritz Dorf–Corviglia funicular was closed for renovation, so check for 2027.
  • The 2017 Bondo landslide area (Val Bondasca) in Bregaglia is closed. Soglio itself is fine.

Worth paying for

  • Bernina Express panorama car≈ CHF 32–44 reservationFloor-to-ceiling windows, but regional trains on the same line are free with a ticket.
  • Rafting the Rhine Gorge≈ CHF 120–150Whitewater through the Ruinaulta.
  • Hotel Waldhaus Sils dinner≈ CHF 80–100Old-world grand hotel atmosphere.
