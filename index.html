Our recommendation

If we had to choose

For a first trip with 5 people who love nature and want to keep costs down, our favourite is mid to late June 2027 using one of the 14-day combos below. Two or three bases means fewer check-ins, cheaper apartments and more variety.

If you only pick one

Bernese Oberland

The Bernese Oberland is the Switzerland everyone dreams of, and it is easy from Langenfeld. Add Lucerne or Ticino to make it 12–14 days.

Best value

Ticino

Ticino (free transport with your stay, great in May) or Appenzell (Option 07, closest and cheapest).

Most epic two weeks

Grand Train Tour

The Grand Train Tour by train, or the Alpine Road Trip (Option 09) if someone is happy to drive.

When to go

May or June?

May cheaper · greener · snow up high

Shoulder season: lower prices, fewer tourists, waterfalls at their peak and blossoming meadows. But many high trails above ~1,800 m are still snowy and some lifts are closed for maintenance. Best regions: Ticino, Lake Geneva, Lucerne, Appenzell, Italian lakes. Watch out for the long weekends (Ascension 6 May, Pentecost 16–17 May), when Swiss and Germans travel too.

June everything opens

From mid-June nearly all lifts and passes are open, the days are the longest of the year and the high-alpine lakes thaw. It is still before the July–August school holidays. Sweet spot: Sat 12 – Sat 26 June 2027. Late June is ideal for Zermatt, Engadin and the road trip.

Getting there

Langenfeld → Switzerland

Langenfeld sits on the S6 between Köln and Düsseldorf, which have direct ICEs to Basel. For a group of 5 the train is the best balance of price, comfort and time. A car wins only for the road trip.

🚆
Train (recommended)5–8 h
€30–80 pp each way if booked early

Langenfeld (Rheinl) → Köln or Düsseldorf Hbf (S6/RE, 15–20 min) → ICE to Basel SBB (≈ 4 h) → anywhere in Switzerland. Book DB 'Sparpreis Europa' / 'Super Sparpreis Europa' (from €19.99–24.99). International tickets open 6 months ahead. Domestic tickets to Basel Bad Bf (the German station in Basel) open 12 months ahead and are often cheapest. From there it is a 5-minute hop to Basel SBB.

🚗
Car6–7 h
≈ €20–60 pp each way (fuel ÷ 5) + CHF 40 vignette

Best for the Road Trip (Option 09) and hidden corners. Five adults with two weeks of luggage need a 7-seater or a roof box. Buy the e-vignette at via.admin.ch only (CHF 40; the 2027 vignette is valid 1 Dec 2026 – 31 Jan 2028). Parking at the car-free resorts costs ≈ CHF 10–25/day.

🚌
FlixBus9–11 h
≈ €25–40 pp

Köln → Zürich/Basel. The cheapest ticket, but you lose most of a day each way. The train is usually worth the extra €10–30.

✈️
Flights (for your friends)–
depends on origin

Fly into Düsseldorf (DUS) or Köln/Bonn (CGN), both ≈ 30 min from Langenfeld. Consider an open-jaw ticket: arrive at DUS/CGN, fly home from Zürich (ZRH), Geneva (GVA) or Milan (MXP). That saves a 6–8 h journey back. Book long-haul ≈ 2–5 months ahead and European flights 1–3 months ahead, with Google Flights price alerts.

Booking timeline

How far ahead to book

Today is 25 October 2026. Accommodation in the hotspots is the thing to secure first, with free cancellation. Train tickets get more expensive the closer you get. Mountain tickets should be bought last, once you see the weather.

Now (late Oct – Nov 2026)Vote on an option and dates. Book refundable accommodation for hotspots (Lauterbrunnen, Täsch/Zermatt, Lucerne, Lake Como). Friends set flight price alerts. Domestic DB tickets to Basel Bad Bf are already bookable.
6 months before (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)DB international Sparpreis tickets open: buy on day one. SBB Saver Day Passes also go on sale 6 months ahead (limited cheap quotas).
Jan – Feb 2027Friends book flights. Book the rental car (free cancellation) for the road trip. Swiss fares for 2027 will be known (≈ +3–4 % on national tickets from Dec 2026).
~3 months before (Feb – Mar 2027)Glacier Express & Bernina Express seats (reportedly from ~93 days ahead). Mountain huts and guesthouses. Note the SAC huts' new reservation fee from 2027.
2–6 weeks beforeBuy the Swiss Travel Pass / Half Fare Card / regional pass online (fixed price, no early-bird). Check passports. Travel insurance for non-EU friends.
Last weekWatch the forecast. Buy Jungfraujoch/Gornergrat tickets 1–2 days before a clear day. Download offline maps and the SBB, MeteoSwiss and SwitzerlandMobility apps.
Save money

Ten tricks that add up

Rent apartments, cook dinnerFor 5 people an apartment with a kitchen beats hotels by 30–50 %. Coop, Migros, Denner, Aldi and Lidl are everywhere.
Picnic lunchesSupermarket sandwiches, cheese and fruit for CHF 8–12, eaten with a CHF 1 million view.
Free waterDrink from village fountains (unless marked 'kein Trinkwasser').
Guest cardsTicino Ticket, SaastalCard, Engadin 'lifts included', Riviera Card: free transport or lifts just for sleeping there.
Saver Day PassesFrom CHF 29 (with Half Fare) for unlimited travel on long transfer days. Book early.
Regional trains on famous routesSame Bernina/Glacier views, no reservation fee, and windows you can open.
Hike down, ride upBuy one-way lift tickets and walk down. Often the most beautiful part.
Under 25?Youth prices on the Swiss Travel Pass and regional passes are ≈ 30 % cheaper.
Pay in CHFAlways choose to pay in francs (not euros) on card terminals, and use a no-fee card.
Too Good To GoThe app works in Switzerland: bakery and supermarket bags for CHF 4–8.
Practical

Good to know before you go

🔌 PlugsSwiss sockets (type J) take thin 2-pin Euro plugs but NOT German Schuko plugs. Bring 2–3 adapters.
📱 RoamingSwitzerland is not in the EU roaming zone. Check your tariff or buy a Swiss eSIM data plan.
💳 MoneyCHF. Cards and phones work almost everywhere. Keep some cash for mountain huts. Euros are accepted sometimes, at bad rates.
🏥 HealthThe EHIC card works for people with German insurance. Friends from abroad need travel insurance that covers mountain rescue.
⛈️ WeatherJune afternoons bring thunderstorms, so start hikes early. Check MeteoSwiss and the webcams before buying lift tickets.
🥾 TrailsYellow signs = easy paths. White-red-white = mountain trails (sure-footed). Blue-white = alpine (not for you).
🛒 SundaysShops close on Sundays, except at train stations and petrol stations.
🧳 LuggageSBB luggage forwarding (≈ CHF 12–25 per bag) lets you hop between bases with only a daypack.
🛂 DocumentsSchengen rules apply. ETIAS for visa-free visitors is not running yet (Oct 2026) and is expected in 2027, so re-check before travel.
